OUR STORY

Built on experience. Driven by excellence.

From the classroom to a seasoned professional chef and phenomenal businesswoman.

Founded in 2009 by Ms. Xoliswa Ntokazi Nkosi, Ntokazi Catering and Events is a 100% black female-owned enterprise with seventeen years of industry expertise.

What began as a local visionary project has grown into a trusted service provider for KwaZulu-Natal, combining cultural authenticity with professional standards and meticulous attention to detail.

Our team understands that every event carries its own meaning. Whether we are supporting a high-stakes government forum, a corporate gathering, an intimate memorial service or a vibrant traditional wedding, we approach every brief with care, dignity and precision.

Our mission is simple: deliver exceptional culinary experiences that elevate every function into a memorable occasion, without ever compromising on quality, hygiene or service excellence. We're working toward a bigger vision too: to be a respected, influential name in South African hospitality, known as much for our integrity as for our food.

IN HER OWN WORDS

The Ntokazi journey

Before she was plating for government forums and 1,500-guest weddings, Ms. Xoliswa Ntokazi Nkosi was standing in front of a classroom, an educator teaching Hospitality, training young people in the very skills she now practises every day. It's a detail she comes back to often when she's on air: the years spent teaching gave her the structure and standards that now run through everything Ntokazi Catering & Events does, from a boardroom lunch to a family memorial.

In 2009, she took that classroom discipline and turned it into a business, starting small, building relationships one function at a time, and slowly earning the trust of clients who needed more than good food; they needed someone reliable. Seventeen years later, that same passion has grown into a catering and events partner trusted across KwaZulu-Natal, from Transnet's canteens to the KZN Office of the Premier's VIP functions.

It's a story Ms. Nkosi has shared on-air with both Vuma 103FM and Highway Radio: how passion, discipline and a willingness to start small can grow into something people rely on. Have a look through the moments below.

GIVING BACK

Corporate Social Responsibility

Ntokazi Catering & Events is committed to contributing toward the KZN economic community through employment, upskilling and fair, compliant practice.

Growth means little to us if it doesn't create opportunity for others. We're proud to provide real employment to youth and members of the KwaZulu-Natal community. On a permanent basis we employ a core team of over 15 staff, scaling up to 50+ casual staff for larger functions, giving flexible, meaningful work to people across the region.

We operate in full compliance with South African labour legislation, and we invest continuously in our people. Ongoing training and upskilling ensures our team meets industry standards for hygiene, service and hospitality excellence. Many of our supplier relationships have lasted years, built on trust, consistency and mutual respect, and it's that same pride in doing things properly that we bring to every table we serve.
