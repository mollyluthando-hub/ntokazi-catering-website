Our story

Built on experience. Driven by excellence.

Excellence is not an act but a habit.

Founded in 2009 by Ms. Xoliswa Ntokazi Nkosi, Ntokazi Catering and Events is a 100% black female-owned enterprise with seventeen years of industry expertise. From the classroom to a seasoned professional chef and phenomenal businesswoman.

What began as a local visionary project has grown into a trusted service provider for KwaZulu-Natal, combining cultural authenticity with professional standards and meticulous attention to detail.

Every event carries its own meaning. Whether it is a high-stakes government forum, a corporate gathering, an intimate memorial service or a vibrant traditional wedding, we approach every brief with care, dignity and precision, without compromising on quality, hygiene or service excellence.

Giving back

Corporate social responsibility

Growth means little to us if it does not create opportunity for others. We are proud to provide real employment to youth and members of the KwaZulu-Natal community. On a permanent basis we employ a core team of over 15 staff, scaling up to 50+ casual staff for larger functions.

We operate in full compliance with South African labour legislation and invest continuously in our people. Ongoing training and upskilling ensures our team meets industry standards for hygiene, service and hospitality excellence. Many of our supplier relationships have lasted years, built on trust, consistency and mutual respect.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer Halaal dishes?

Yes. We offer Halaal dishes, outsourced from a Halaal-compliant vendor, so every guest at your table is catered for.

Can I customise a menu or package?

Absolutely. Every menu and package can be tailored to your needs, from the menu to the guest count and add-ons.

What size events do you cater for?

From an intimate 20-guest memorial to a 1,500-guest corporate function, without losing the personal touch.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve events across KwaZulu-Natal. Get in touch and we will be happy to discuss your location.

Do your catering prices include labour and groceries?

Our catering price list, from 20 to 200 guests, includes labour and groceries. For a customised quote, send us your event details.

How do I book?

Use the quote form on this page or WhatsApp us on 066 297 2750. The Ntokazi team will get back to you with a tailored quote.

Can I order Café Sizzle outside the delivery radius?

Yes. Café Sizzle is on Uber Eats and Mr D, and if you are outside the delivery radius you can order direct from our kitchen through this website or on WhatsApp.
