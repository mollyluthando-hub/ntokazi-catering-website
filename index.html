Our story

Built on experience. Driven by excellence.

From the classroom to the professional kitchen, and on to building a business.

Founded in 2009 by Ms. Xoliswa Ntokazi Nkosi, Ntokazi Catering and Events is a 100% Black female-owned enterprise with seventeen years of industry expertise. Ms. Nkosi began her career as a Home Economics educator, then furthered her studies and became a professional culinary chef, combining the rigour of the classroom with the artistry of the professional kitchen.

What began as a local vision has grown into a trusted service provider for KwaZulu-Natal, combining cultural authenticity with professional standards and meticulous attention to detail.

Our team understands that every event carries its own meaning. Whether we are supporting a high-stakes government forum, a corporate gathering, an intimate memorial service or a vibrant traditional wedding, we approach every brief with care, dignity and precision.

Our mission is simple: to deliver exceptional culinary experiences that elevate every function into a memorable occasion, without ever compromising on quality, hygiene or service excellence. We are working toward a bigger vision too: to be a respected, influential name in South African hospitality, known as much for our integrity as for our food.

In her own words

The Ntokazi journey

Before she was plating for government forums and 1,500-guest functions, Ms. Xoliswa Ntokazi Nkosi was standing in front of a classroom as a Home Economics educator, training young people in the very skills she now practises every day. She went on to further her studies and became a professional culinary chef. It is a story she returns to often when she is on air: the years spent teaching gave her the structure and standards that now run through everything Ntokazi Catering & Events does, from a boardroom lunch to a family memorial.

In 2009, she took that classroom discipline and turned it into a business, starting small, building relationships one function at a time, and steadily earning the trust of clients who needed more than good food; they needed someone reliable. Seventeen years later, that same passion has grown into a catering and events partner trusted across KwaZulu-Natal, from Transnet’s canteens to the KZN Office of the Premier’s VIP functions.

Ms. Nkosi has shared her story on air with both Vuma 103FM and Highway Radio: how passion, discipline and a willingness to start small can grow into something people rely on. Have a look through the moments below.

Giving back

Corporate Social Responsibility

Ntokazi Catering & Events is committed to contributing toward the KZN economic community through employment, upskilling and fair, compliant practice.

Growth means little to us if it does not create opportunity for others. We are proud to provide real employment to youth and members of the KwaZulu-Natal community. On a permanent basis we employ a core team of over 15 staff, scaling up to 50+ casual staff for larger functions, giving flexible, meaningful work to people across the region.

We operate in full compliance with South African labour legislation, and we invest continuously in our people. Ongoing training and upskilling ensure our team meets industry standards for hygiene, service and hospitality excellence. Many of our supplier relationships have lasted years, built on trust, consistency and mutual respect, and it is that same pride in doing things properly that we bring to every table we serve.
